About

When a surprising benchmark result appears, Ali finds out whether it is a real model change or a measurement error — before anyone acts on it. As LLM Research Staff and Tech Lead, Model Evaluation at Multiverse Computing, he leads evaluation across teams so release decisions rest on the same evidence, and develops compression and distillation methods that make language models practical to deploy. Previously at BIFOLD and TU Berlin, he built foundation models and low-latency inference systems for biomedical and vision-language applications, holding a Ph.D. summa cum laude in Computer Science and Machine Learning. His work is backed by 10+ publications at venues including NeurIPS and IEEE TMI.

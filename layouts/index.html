About

Ali is a Principal LLM Researcher at Multiverse Computing, where he works on making large language models smaller, faster, and cheaper to run without sacrificing accuracy. He holds a Ph.D. summa cum laude in Computer Science and Machine Learning from Technische Universität Berlin, and spent three years as a Machine Learning Research Scientist at BIFOLD and Postdoctoral Research Fellow at TU Berlin, building foundation models, diffusion models, and efficient inference pipelines for biomedical and vision-language applications. His research spans representation learning (distillation, sparse autoencoders), parameter-efficient fine-tuning, sparsity, and Bayesian optimization, resulting in 10+ publications at venues including NeurIPS and IEEE TMI. He has supervised 5+ M.Sc. and Ph.D. students and taught courses to 200+ participants.

Research Interests

  • Foundation Models & LLM Efficiency
  • Distillation, PEFT & Sparsity
  • Diffusion & Generative Models
  • Bayesian Optimization
  • Representation Learning
  • Multimodal & Vision-Language AI

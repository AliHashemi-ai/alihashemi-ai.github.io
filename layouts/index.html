About

Ali is a Senior LLM Researcher on the LLM Research Staff at Multiverse Computing, and Tech Lead for Model Evaluation. He leads evaluation so teams ship on evidence, builds AI tooling that makes that work reproducible, and develops post-training methods — distillation and recovery of compressed models — that help ship smaller production LLMs. Previously at BIFOLD and TU Berlin, he built foundation models and low-latency inference systems for biomedical and vision-language applications, holding a Ph.D. summa cum laude in Computer Science and Machine Learning. His work is backed by 10+ publications at venues including NeurIPS and IEEE TMI.

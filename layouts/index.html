About

Ali builds and ships efficient large language models — from research prototypes to production systems. As Principal LLM Researcher at Multiverse Computing, he works on compressing and optimizing LLMs to cut inference cost without sacrificing accuracy. Previously at BIFOLD and TU Berlin, he built foundation models, diffusion models, and low-latency inference pipelines for biomedical and vision-language applications, holding a Ph.D. summa cum laude in Computer Science and Machine Learning. His work spans distillation, PEFT, sparsity, and generative modeling, backed by 10+ publications at venues including NeurIPS and IEEE TMI.

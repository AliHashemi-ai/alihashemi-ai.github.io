About

Ali is a Senior LLM Researcher and Tech Lead at Multiverse Computing, leading LLM evaluation across research, product, services and MLOps on one shared platform, so release decisions and regression checks rest on the same evidence. He builds the evaluation infrastructure, benchmarks and AI tooling behind that work, including agentic and coding-agent evaluation and automatic checkpoint scoring, and develops post training methods, including distillation and compression recovery, that ship smaller production LLMs. Previously at BIFOLD and TU Berlin, he built foundation models and low-latency inference systems for biomedical and vision-language applications, holding a Ph.D. (Distinction / summa cum laude) in trustworthy machine learning. His work is backed by 10+ publications at venues including NeurIPS and IEEE TMI.

About

Ali is a Principal LLM Researcher at Multiverse Computing, where he works on compressing and optimizing large language models to cut inference cost without sacrificing accuracy.

Previously at BIFOLD and Technische Universität Berlin, he built foundation models, diffusion models, and low-latency inference pipelines for biomedical and vision-language applications, holding a Ph.D. summa cum laude in Computer Science and Machine Learning.

He works primarily in Python and PyTorch, with vLLM, TensorRT, Hugging Face, LangChain, Docker, Kubernetes, and AWS/GCP for production deployment.

Research

Efficiency

How small can an LLM get without losing accuracy?

Compression, distillation, and sparsity methods that cut model size and inference cost while preserving task performance.

Generative Modeling

Diffusion & consistency models for scientific data

Conditional diffusion models for biomedical inverse problems, accelerated with distilled consistency models for near single-step sampling.

Representation Learning

Interpretable compression via distillation

Subspace-based distillation and sparse autoencoders for building compact, interpretable foundation models from large teacher models.

Optimization

Bayesian & nonconvex optimization at scale

Large-scale optimization algorithms combining sparse Bayesian statistics and machine learning for high-dimensional inverse problems.

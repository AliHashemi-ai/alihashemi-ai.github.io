About

Ali is a Senior LLM Researcher and Tech Lead at Multiverse Computing. He leads LLM evaluation, including frontier model evaluation, from benchmark and experimental design to release gates, and catches scoring errors before they are treated as model regressions. For European-language evaluation, he builds LLM-as-judge scoring and automated graders, and curates eval datasets. He also designs and leads agentic AI coding workflows, builds eval harnesses and contributes to an agent harness. His quality gates check AI-written code before it ships. He also works on continued pretraining and distributed training of large mixture-of-experts LLMs, including multi-token prediction, long-context training and multi-node parallelism on H200 and B300 clusters. Post-training covers SFT, RLHF, DPO, GRPO and multi-teacher distillation. He contributed SFT on HyperNova 60B and develops quantization-aware healing and PEFT so compressed models reach production quality. Multiverse Computing's public Pulsar 16B model card also reports SFT after compression. Previously at BIFOLD, he built an interpretable distillation method that cut foundation model size by 80% with 1% accuracy loss. He holds a Ph.D. (Distinction / summa cum laude) from TU Berlin and has 10+ publications, including NeurIPS and IEEE TMI.
